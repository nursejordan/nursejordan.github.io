🦸🏾‍♀️ Leading Through Code — The Week Playbook

Owning Your Superpower: one week from gamer → game developer → leader. This is the whole camp at a glance — every workshop, lab, and link, colour-coded like the rest of the kit.

What you'll accomplish

Junior Designers · 10–13

Learn how games actually work, deep-diving the design process with Threadbare.

Senior Engineers · 14–17

Code a fully working 2D platformer from scratch in Godot — the professional lifecycle.

The Ultimate Challenge

Everyone: turn your own original idea into a live, launch-ready platformer.

Team Superhero Comic

In groups of 3–4: write, design & publish a superhero comic with real world-building.

Your milestones (how you win the week)

  1. Technical Mastery — finish the full 10-session curriculum (Threadbare 10–13 / Godot 14–17).
  2. Collaborative Leadership — publish your group's Superhero Comic in Canva, weaving in the morning leadership frameworks.
  3. Advanced Microcredential (optional, 14–17) — 5 extra Threadbare cross-training sessions.
  4. Creative Innovation Challenge (optional) — custom mechanics, secret levels, or your own scoring system.

Push through Steps 8–12: the Supreme Ordeal (your heroes' combined superpowers vs. their greatest challenge), intentionally defeat a real-world stereotype, and Return with the Elixir — home, changed, ready to lift their community.

THUPlaytesting, Feedback & Presentation Prep▶
Question of the dayRecall a moment you felt nervous about a brand-new challenge. What inner strength pushed you through — and how does that courage make you a leader who inspires others?

LeadershipWorkshop 4 · Inspiring Girls to Go For It

Ideas + skills need one more thing: the courage to step into the spotlight. Practice speaking confidently about your work and turning this week into real-world action.

CodingMorning Lab · QA & Peer Playtesting

  • Hour 1 — The Polish: final touches on terrain, sound levels, animations.
  • Hour 2 — The Exchange: swap laptops! Hunt for bugs, broken collisions, unclear paths in each other's games.
  • The Feedback Loop: fill the official Playtest Feedback Form — constructive, clear, kind.

CodingAfternoon · Feedback → Polish + Slides

  • Rapid prototyping: fix at least one major issue your playtester found.
  • Developer Spotlight slide: your concept, code snippets, Piskel art, and growth story.
  • Superhero Team pitch deck: lore, Canva art pages, and how the Hero's Journey showcases real leadership.

ShowcaseEvening · Dress Rehearsal (Science-Fair format)

Stations set: game loaded on one screen, slides on another, one device showing the team comic. Room splits into two shifts — Shift A presents while Shift B tours and asks questions, then swap after 20 minutes.
