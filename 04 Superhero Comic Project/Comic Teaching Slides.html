The Superhero Comic

Your team. Your heroes. Your story — published by Friday.

The mission

By Friday, your team will publish…

a real comic where the heroes' powers are YOUR real strengths — and the villain is a stereotype you smash.

Step 1

Teams of 3–4 · everyone leads something

✍️ Story Leadscript & dialogue
🎨 Art Directorstyle & characters
🖥️ Canva Leadlayout & assembly
📚 Lore Keeperworld rules & continuity
Everyone draws AND writes. A role means you lead that part.
Step 2 · the secret rule

Real superpowers → comic superpowers

💗Empathy → she feels what others feel, from miles away
📋Organization → she can freeze chaos into perfect order
😂Humor → her jokes literally disarm villains
🧩Problem-solving → she sees the hidden path in any trap

Fill in your Character Sheet: name, power, look, weakness, catchphrase.

Step 3

Build a world with RULES

1Name the world + the setting
2Write 3 rules — what's possible, what's forbidden
3Invent the villain = a real-world stereotype in disguise
Rules make it real. A world where anything goes is a world nobody believes.

Star Wars, Moana, Spider-Man — same 12 steps. Now it's yours.

Writing tip

Show, don't tell

✗"She was brave."
✓Draw her stepping forward while everyone else backs away.
Checkpoint: point to one panel where a real superpower makes the choice.
The climax

Defeat the stereotype

8The Ordeal: the team wins only by combining their different powers
11Resurrection: the villain's lie is disproven — on the page
12The Elixir: the heroes come home and lift their community
Building it

Paper first, Canva second

1Sketch each page on a Page Planner — stick figures are perfect
2Canva → Templates → search "comic book template" → pick a FREE one
3Rebuild your sketch: panels, art, speech bubbles (Elements → "speech bubble")
4Share the design with your whole team + facilitator
⚠️ Watch for the crown icon — that means paid. Free only! · Max 2 fonts for the whole comic.
Finish line

Polish → Publish → Present

✓Read it out loud as a team — fix what's confusing
✓All 12 steps visible in the pages?
✓Share → Download as PDF + copy the view link
✓Friday: every teammate introduces HER hero — 60 seconds each
