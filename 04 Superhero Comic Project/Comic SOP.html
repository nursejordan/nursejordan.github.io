🦸🏾‍♀️ SOP — The Team Superhero Comic

In groups of 3–4: write, design & publish a superhero comic with real world-building — where every hero's power is a real leadership superpower from your team. Eight steps across the week.

The deliverable: a published Canva comic (6–10 pages) that follows the 12-step Hero's Journey, stars heroes powered by your team's real strengths, and defeats a real-world stereotype in its climax. Presented at Friday's showcase.
1

Form your team & pick roles

Mon · Lab 1
  1. Groups of 3–4. Everyone draws AND writes — roles just mean you lead that part.
  2. Pick one lead each: Story Lead (script & dialogue) · Art Director (style & characters) · Canva Lead (layout & assembly) · Lore Keeper (world rules & continuity — in a team of 3, Story Lead takes this).
  3. Fill in the Team Roster sheet — team name included!
📄 Sheet 1 · Team Roster

These are Friday's career cards in miniature: Creative Director, Technical Artist, Project Manager. Say that out loud — it lands.

2

Turn real superpowers into heroes

Mon · Lab 1
  1. From this morning's workshop: each girl names her real-life superpower (empathy, organization, humor, problem-solving, art…).
  2. Each girl builds her hero on a Character Sheet: hero name, the real strength → comic power, costume/look, a weakness (heroes need one!), and a catchphrase.
  3. Optional: use Google Gemini to brainstorm names and lore — the ideas stay yours.
📄 Sheet 2 · Character Sheet (one per girl)

The rule that makes it work: the comic power must be the real strength in disguise. Empathy → feels what others feel from miles away. Organization → can freeze chaos into order.

3

Build the world

Mon · Lab 1
  1. As a team, fill the World-Building sheet: world name, the setting (city? reef? space school?), and 3 rules of the world (what's possible here? what's forbidden?).
  2. Invent the villain — and here's the twist: the villain embodies a real-world stereotype your team wants to smash ("girls can't code," "leaders must be loud"…).
  3. Watch: What is the Hero's Journey?
📄 Sheet 3 · World-Building
4

Map the journey — steps 1–4

Mon · Lab 1

Use the Hero's Journey Planner — one sentence per step is enough today. The 12 steps, split across the week:

Monday · 1–4
  1. Ordinary World — home, normal life
  2. Call to Adventure — something changes
  3. Refusal — doubt & fear
  4. Meeting the Mentor — a guide appears
Tuesday · 4–8
  1. Crossing the Threshold — no turning back
  2. Tests, Allies & Enemies
  3. Approach the Inmost Cave — the big one nears
  4. The Ordeal — the hardest fight begins
Wednesday · 8–12
  1. Reward — what she gains
  2. The Road Back
  3. Resurrection — the final test, biggest change
  4. Return with the Elixir — home, changed, sharing the gift
📄 Sheet 4 · Hero's Journey Planner
5

Write the middle — powers into dialogue

Tue · Lab 2
  1. Advance through stages 4–8: cross the threshold, meet allies, face the first big tests.
  2. The assignment: write each girl's real leadership superpower directly into her hero's choices and dialogue — not just the powers, the decisions.
  3. Show, don't tell: instead of "she was brave," draw her stepping forward when everyone backs away.

Checkpoint question for each team: "point to one panel where a real superpower made the choice."

6

Climax & resolution — defeat the stereotype

Wed · Lab 3
  1. The Supreme Ordeal: the heroes face the villain — and win only by combining their different superpowers.
  2. Defeat the stereotype: the villain's lie gets disproven on the page, by characters proud of who they are.
  3. Return with the Elixir: the heroes come home changed — show how they use what they learned to lift their community.
  4. Script complete = all 12 planner boxes filled with real sentences.
7

Build it in Canva

Mon→Thu · ongoing
  1. Canva Lead: open Canva → Templates ▶ 0:10 and search "comic book template" ▶ 0:20.
  2. Pick a free template whose panel layout matches your pages ▶ 0:28, open it ▶ 0:43.
  3. Fill panels with the team's art + Canva elements ▶ 1:24 — use the Page Planner sheet to sketch each page on paper FIRST, then rebuild it in Canva (way faster than designing live).
  4. Speech bubbles: Elements → search "speech bubble." Keep fonts to two max for the whole comic.
  5. Share the design with all teammates + your facilitator (Share → invite by email) so everyone can work on it.
📄 Sheet 5 · Page Planner (one per page)
⚠️ Watch out
  • Free vs. paid: some templates/elements cost money — check for the crown icon before using.
  • Only use approved art — your drawings, Canva's free elements, or licensed assets.
  • Consistency: same style, same fonts, same colours across every page.
8

Polish, publish & present

Thu–Fri
  1. Thursday polish: read the whole comic out loud as a team — fix confusing panels, typos, missing steps. Check all 12 journey points are visible in the pages.
  2. Publish: Canva → Share → Download as PDF (and grab a public view link).
  3. The pitch deck: add your comic pages to the team presentation slides — hero lore, the stereotype you defeated, and which real superpowers powered the story.
  4. Friday showcase: one device at your station shows the comic. Every teammate speaks — introduce YOUR hero and the real strength behind her.

Rehearse Thursday evening in the dress-rehearsal rotation — 60 seconds per girl is plenty.

Companion pieces: the Worksheet Pack (Sheets 1–5, printable) and the Comic Slides deck. Canva mechanics follow the camp's Loom video (Recipe #1 in the SOP Book). Journey mapping matches the playbook: steps 1–4 Mon · 4–8 Tue · 8–12 Wed.
