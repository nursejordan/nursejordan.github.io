🐠 Reef Runner — Mod Challenges

Dares for your game. Do one, then hit Play and watch!

How to play: click the piece it says → change the number or colour in the Settings panel → press ▶ Play (F5). Don't like it? Undo with Ctrl/⌘ + Z and try another.
⭐

🌙 Moon Jump

Click Player → Jump Force
Change it to −900.
She launches super high! (Keep it negative.)
⭐

🐌 Slow-Mo Splash

Click Player → Gravity
Change it to 400.
She floats down slowly, like she's underwater.
⭐

⚡ Speedy Fish

Click Player → Speed
Change it to 500.
Zoom! She runs twice as fast.
⭐

🔥 Lava Floor

Click Ground → Visual → Color
Pick a bright red or orange.
The sea floor turns to lava!
⭐⭐

🌈 Rainbow Reefs

Click a Coin → Sprite2D → Modulate
Give each reef a different colour.
A rainbow reef to save!
⭐⭐

🌀 Dizzy Reefs

Click a Coin → Spin Speed
Change it to 12.
The reefs spin like tops.
⭐⭐

👹 Super Enemy

Click Enemy1 → Speed & Move Distance
Speed 300, Move Distance 400.
It zooms way farther. Can you dodge it?
⭐

🐱 Nine Lives

Click GameManager → Starting Lives
Change it to 9.
Way more tries before Game Over.
⭐⭐

💥 Reef Explosion

Right-click Coin1 → Duplicate
Make 10 copies and scatter them in the Stage.
A whole reef to rescue — each one already works!
⭐⭐

🏝️ Build Your Level

Drag Platform1 / Platform2 in the Stage
Move them into a staircase she can climb.
You just designed a level!
⭐⭐⭐

🎨 Reskin the Hero

Files panel → replace icon.svg
Drop in your own drawing (make one in Piskel!).
Every fish, reef & enemy uses YOUR art.
