Now we build

7 small steps

1 Open Godot
2 Make the character
3 Picture + bump-shape
4 Teach the keys
5 Give her a brain
6 Add the ground
7 Press GO 🎉
Step 1 of 7
Top bar

Open Godot

  • From scratch: click + New Project → name it → Create & Edit.
  • Camp gave you one: click Import → open its project.godot → Import & Edit.
Look for: the 4 colour areas appear. You're home.
Step 2 of 7
Parts list

Make the character

  • Add node → search CharacterBody2D → Create.
  • Rename it Player.
Why: this is the body a player controls that bumps into walls & floors.
Step 3 of 7
Parts list + Settings

Picture + bump-shape

  • Right-click Player → Add Child → Sprite2D = her picture. (No art? use icon.svg.)
  • Add Child → CollisionShape2D = her bump-shape. Give it a Rectangle.
Step 4 of 7
Top bar

Teach Godot the keys

  • Project → Project Settings → Input Map.
  • Add 3 actions, spelled exactly:
    move_left · move_right · jump
  • Give each a key: ← → and Space.
Spelling matters! lowercase, with the underscore.
Step 5 of 7
Parts list → Script

Give her a brain

Right-click Player → Attach Script. In plain words, every moment it says:

# fall if not on the ground
# slide left/right with the arrows
# jump if Space + on the ground
# flip to face the way she walks
move_and_slide()
Step 6 of 7
Parts list

Give her ground

  • New scene → Node2D, name it Level.
  • Add a StaticBody2D + CollisionShape2D = the floor.
  • Drag Player in, above the floor.
Why: without a floor, she falls forever!
Step 7 of 7
GO

Press ▶ and play!

  • Press ▶ Play (or F5). Pick Level as the main scene.
  • Arrows to walk. Space to jump.
Totally normal

When it breaks — that's game-making

A Falls through floor? → both need a bump-shape.
B Won't move? → check the key spelling.
C Flies away? → jump_force must be negative.
You did it 🎉
