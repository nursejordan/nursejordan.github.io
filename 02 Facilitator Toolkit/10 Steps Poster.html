🐠 Build a Platformer in 10 Steps

Every game you love was made exactly like this.

1🎨

Get your art

Grab the sprites · make pixels crisp (Filter → Nearest)

2🧱

Make your tiles

TileMapLayer + your tile picture = building blocks

3🖌️

Paint your level

Draw floors & platforms like a coloring book

4🐟

Build the Player

Body + picture + bump-shape + camera — the family of 4

5🎞️

Load her poses

The sprite sheet = all her moves in one picture

6⌨️

Teach the keys

left · right · jump — spelled exactly

7🧠

Brain 1: walk & fall

Arrows move her · gravity pulls her down

8🚀

Brain 2: jump

Space + on the floor · remember: negative = UP!

9✨

Brain 3: smooth moves

Speed up slowly, glide to a stop — the pro feel

10🎬

Animate + press GO!

Idle, run, jump, fall — then ▶ and CHEER 🎉
