🐠 Reef Runner — Facilitator Setup

Everything to do before the girls arrive. ~15 min the first time, ~5 min after.

once

A · Set up the computers

If Godot won't open (macOS warning): right-click the Godot app → Open → Open. Or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. You only do this once.
once

B · Print the handouts

  • ☐ Cheat Sheet — 1 per girl
  • ☐ Bookmarks — 1 sheet per 4 girls, then cut
  • ☐ Coloring Page — 1 per girl
  • ☐ Mod Challenge Cards — 1 per girl (or per pair)
  • ☐ Facilitator Script — 1 for you
  • ☐ Guide — 1 for you (optional, per girl)

All print from the PDFs in your Drive folder or Desktop. Turn double-sided OFF for the bookmarks.

day-of

C · Run the session (in order)

  1. Warm-up: hand out the coloring page as they arrive.
  2. Slides on the projector — press F for full screen.
  3. Teach the 4-colour map (slides 3–8). Go slow here.
  4. Give out cheat sheets + bookmarks.
  5. Either build the 7 steps together, or open the template and mod it.
  6. Finishers get the Mod Challenge cards.
remember

D · Your job, in one line

You don't teach Godot — you point at the 5 colours and let the girls click.
